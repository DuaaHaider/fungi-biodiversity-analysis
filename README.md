**Fungal Biodiversity Analysis Project Overview**
This project investigates the effects of abiotic factors on fungal fruiting by comparing mushroom abundance to soil moisture levels in shaded and unshaded environments. This analysis explores how environmental variation may influence fungal communities, with a focus on soil moisture and solar exposure as potential drivers of fungal abundance. 
(Data obtained as part of a self-designed experiment)

**Research Question** The influence of sunlight exposure and soil moisture levels on fungal fruiting 

**Methods** Data was collected from shaded and unshaded field sites located on University of York Campus.

**Statistical analysis**
- Welch two-sample t-test
- data visualisation with ggplot2
